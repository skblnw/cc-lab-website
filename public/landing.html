// mission.txt

Sequence is language. Structure is physics.

// research_directions.map

Four lines of inquiry, one intersection.

[01] · structural_fm

Structural Bioinformatics & Foundation Models

How can we decode the language of protein structure and function at scale?

Reading the grammar of proteins from evolutionary-scale data to infer function and design molecules.

Protein structure & function prediction Biological sequence foundation models Structure-informed generative design
[02] · biophysics_md

Computational Biophysics & Molecular Dynamics

How do molecular motions govern biological function and drug action?

Putting proteins in motion to expose the hidden states, allosteric switches, and binding energetics.

MD simulation & enhanced sampling Allostery & conformational dynamics Binding free energy calculations
[03] · precision_med

Precision Medicine & Immunotherapy

How can we tailor therapies to individual molecular and genomic profiles?

Reading each individual's molecular profile to identify patient-specific vulnerabilities and predict treatment response.

Neoantigen prediction & vaccine design Treatment response & patient stratification mRNA therapeutics optimization
[04] · dynamics_dd

Dynamics-Based Drug Discovery

How can we exploit protein dynamics and targeted protein degradation to find next-generation therapeutics?

Drugging the "undruggable" by opening transient pockets and recruiting the cell's own machinery to destroy disease targets.

Cryptic pocket & allosteric drug discovery Molecular glues & targeted protein degradation PROTAC & bifunctional molecule design
